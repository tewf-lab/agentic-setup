# Position: where the review ends and the work begins

What the field has shown, what these searches did not find, and what this repository asks next.

**Shape:** [position](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics-review/blob/master/shapes/position.md)

## Known

- Assignment under cost and capacity carries proven approximations (Lenstra 1990; Shmoys 1993).
- Algorithms that take advice carry guarantees without a distribution (Mitzenmacher 2022).
- Learned cascades and routers cut cost by a large factor at equal quality, on routing benchmarks (Chen 2023; Ong 2024; Kotte 2026).
- A rate from a small sample is estimated with binomial intervals (Sauro 2005; Ross 2003).
- A learned policy beat a constraint solver at the same time limit on very large job-shop instances (Tassel 2023).

## Not found in these searches

- F4, arxiv, FrugalGPT: no work applying a cascade to a DAG of agent jobs over rounds.
- F11, serper-scholar: no learned scheduler with a guarantee on the same jobs as its exact rival.
- F12, serper-scholar: no study of an agent's success rate per class from few observations.
- F9 and F10 failed, so one engine of the formal plan and the whole of OpenAIRE were never searched.

## The questions

| Question | Answer from the evidence | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | the assignment is solved and bounded; uncertainty enters through the estimates, not through the solver | supported with caveat |
| Q2 | learned policies win at equal time on large instances, and carry no guarantee found here | measured |
| Q3 | routing and cascades are the practice, measured on benchmarks rather than on a job ledger | measured |
| Q4 | binomial intervals and calibration exist, and nothing on agents per class | measured, descriptive |

## What this repository asks

- How much does the exact flow lose when its pass rates are wrong by the width of a small-sample interval?
- Does a learned policy beat the flow on this hub's ledger at the same dollar budget, on cost per passed job?

## What the shapes must decide

- Whether a second rater rescores the grey sources, and whether a private repository may stand as a source at all.
- Whether a review whose two resting reviews are private should name them, or only point at them.
