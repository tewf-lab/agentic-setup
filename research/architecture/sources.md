## Left out, with the reason

- The travel-planning case of the LLM-Modulo family, found at F4: the framework paper and its evaluation together suffice.
- Two variants of the issue-resolving benchmark, a live one and a Java one, found at F7: the original carries the concept.
- Eight titles of F12, training variants of the instruction hierarchy: one kept work of that family covers the idea.
- Three titles of F2, a recommender benchmark, a failure-attribution framework and an uncertainty paper: aside from control.
- Two titles of F6, a Java code generator and a database-aggregate oracle: generation and testing without agents in the loop.
- Three titles of F5, a multi-agent reinforcement-learning survey, a 2013 simulation methodology and a spectroscopic survey: reached by word match alone.
- Twenty-one titles in all were located and not read; every one was screened by title at its string.
