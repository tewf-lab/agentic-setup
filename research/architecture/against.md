# Against: the evidence that cuts the other way

What would change this review's answers, and where the pool disagrees with itself.

**Shape:** [against](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics-review/blob/master/shapes/against.md)

## Against "the enforced hook settles control"

- Mediation is only as good as the mediator: specialised guards' false positives rose by up to 47 points across surface forms (Demirkiran 2026, abstract; secondary).
- Automatic permission review stopped none of the 13 objectives on the 3 harnesses offering that mode, so a review layer is not a monitor (He 2026, abstract; secondary).
- The gate loads at session start and holds in one folder, so sessions opened elsewhere run unguarded (Tewf 2026 hub, its own statement of limits; unverifiable).
- Trained priority is the rival answer: a hierarchy inside the model raised robustness even for attack types unseen in training (Wallace 2024, abstract; secondary).

## Against "tests as acceptance is enough"

- Soundness belongs to the verifier set, and repository tests are not proven sound; a guarantee of correct outputs needs complete verifiers (Gundawar 2024, abstract; secondary).
- The best model of its year resolved 1.96 percent of real issues, so passing is rare and a partial suite is seductive (Jimenez 2024, abstract; secondary).
- Tests encode what their author foresaw: security regressions survived functional suites until security tests joined the loop (Wang 2024, abstract; secondary).

## Against "the class table with ceilings is the right economy"

- Learned routing beat fixed reuse of two cascade routers at equal quality, 72 against 31 and 44 percent saved (Paul 2026, abstract; secondary).
- A ceiling obeyed blindly can cost more than a router's mistake, since retries multiply the apparent price up to 4.25 times (Fu 2026, abstract; secondary).

## Disagreements

| Point | One side | Other side |
|---|---|---|
| Where control lives | in the harness, by hooks and guardrails (OpenAI 2025, the guardrails chapter) | in the model, by trained instruction priority (Wallace 2024, abstract; secondary) |
| Whether to use a framework | a graph runtime carries state, persistence and control (LangChain 2026, overview) | begin with direct calls, since layers hide prompts and bugs (Anthropic 2024, when and how to use frameworks) |

- A possible reason for the first split: the model-side result is measured on attacks, while the harness-side guidance rests on deployments that the guide does not share.
